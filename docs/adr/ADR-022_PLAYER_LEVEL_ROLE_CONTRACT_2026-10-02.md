# ADR-022: Player Level role contract

- Status: Proposed for local acceptance
- Date: 2026-10-02
- Layer: L3 progression contract

## Context

ADR-021 introduced the initial Player Level / EXP requirement curve, but Player Level still needed an explicit gameplay role. Wanderloom already has independent power and access axes:

- Equipment modifies frozen exploration character stats.
- Zone Rank controls production-zone availability.
- Zone risk and duration control failure probability.
- Formation and zone risk control Operational Cost.
- Zone, formation and duration control Gold economics.

Adding direct Level-based combat stats or economy multipliers now would couple progression to systems that already have dedicated authorities and would reduce the relative importance of equipment.

## Decision

For the initial production baseline, Player Level is a progression and achievement indicator only.

`resolvePlayerLevelRoleEffects(level)` therefore resolves to neutral effects for every valid Level 1 through 10:

```text
statModifiers                = {}
failureProbabilityMultiplier = 1
goldRewardMultiplier          = 1
zoneRankBonus                 = 0
```

Player Level does not directly:

- add ATK/DEF or other CharacterStats;
- alter expedition failure probability;
- modify Gold reward or Operational Cost;
- unlock or bypass Zone Rank requirements;
- change rarity reachability;
- alter formation balance.

## Rationale

This keeps the initial progression model easy to reason about and preserves the separation of responsibilities established by earlier ADRs.

Equipment remains the principal character-power axis. Zone Rank remains the content-access axis. Player Level provides a visible measure of accumulated progression without silently changing combat or economy outcomes.

This is also compatible with the Solo Competitiveness principle: Level accumulation cannot create a hidden group advantage or force a stat race that dominates low-overhead Solo play.

## Future extension boundary

Future releases may add small Level-derived benefits, but they must be introduced explicitly through this role contract and separately accepted. Candidate future benefits include cosmetic unlocks, profile milestones, loadout convenience, or very small capped utility bonuses.

Any future direct stat, failure-rate, Gold, rarity, or Zone-access effect requires a new balance decision and must not be added implicitly.

## Non-goals

This ADR does not define:

- character stat names or combat formulas;
- Skill systems;
- equipment balance;
- Zone Rank progression rules;
- cosmetic rewards;
- runtime stochastic failure cutover.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Level 1 and Level 10 both resolve to neutral role effects.
2. All Levels 1 through 10 use a failure multiplier of 1.
3. All Levels 1 through 10 use a Gold multiplier of 1.
4. All Levels 1 through 10 use a Zone Rank bonus of 0.
5. No direct CharacterStats modifiers are generated by Level.
6. Values outside the accepted production Level range are rejected.
